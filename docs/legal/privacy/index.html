Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Overview

Gent is a desktop workspace for terminals, coding tools, files, web previews, and paired devices. Gent is designed to work without a Gent account. We do not sell personal information or use Gent to build advertising profiles.

Information stored on your device

Gent stores the information needed to provide its features on the device where you use it. This can include app settings, device and pairing information, terminal and project metadata, conversation history, files you choose to manage or transfer, and local web-preview state. Sensitive pairing and relay credentials are stored through the operating system's secure credential storage when it is available.

You control this information through your operating system and Gent's settings. Removing a workspace, conversation, transferred file, or the app can remove the corresponding local data, subject to any operating-system backup or retention settings you have enabled.

Paired devices and remote access

When you pair devices, Gent exchanges the information needed to identify and authenticate those devices and transmits workspace data between them so that remote features work. Optional remote access may use the relay configuration you enable. Connections use the security mechanisms provided by Gent and the platforms on which it runs.

Third-party services you choose

Gent can connect to coding assistants, MCP connectors, speech-to-text services, and other tools that you configure. When you use one of these services, the prompts, files, credentials, audio, or other information you choose to send may be processed by that provider under its own privacy policy and terms. Gent does not control those providers' handling of information.

Diagnostics and tracking

Gent does not include advertising tracking or a Gent-operated analytics or crash-reporting service. Your operating system, distribution channel, network provider, and any third-party service you use may process information independently under their own policies.

Children

Gent is intended for general productivity and development use. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when Gent changes. The current version and its revision date are published on this page. For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, open an issue in the Gent issue tracker.
